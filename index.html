A billion is not just a big million

When politicians and journalists talk about billionaires, the numbers can feel abstract — just very large figures in a news story. But the difference between a million dollars and a billion dollars is not a matter of degree. It is a matter of kind.

If you spent $1,000 every single day, it would take you nearly three years to spend a million dollars. To spend a billion at the same rate, you would need almost three thousand years. The Roman Empire rose and fell in less time than it would take to exhaust a billion dollars at a thousand dollars a day.

This gap matters because wealth at that scale confers a qualitatively different kind of power — over investment decisions, over political speech, over the shape of public life. Understanding the scale is the first step toward thinking clearly about it.
