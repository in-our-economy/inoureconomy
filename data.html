Data & Visualizations

Charts, datasets, and interactive figures

Income Share of the Top 1%

Pre-tax national income share held by the top 1% in the United States, 1913–2019. Data from the World Inequality Database.

Source: World Inequality Database (wid.world)

Wage Growth by Income Percentile

Cumulative percent change in real annual wages, 1979–2022, by wage percentile. Lower-wage workers have seen lower cumulative wage growth over this period.

Source: Economic Policy Institute analysis of Current Population Survey data

Federal Budget Composition

Share of federal outlays by major category, FY 2023.

Source: Congressional Budget Office

Unemployment Rate by Education

Annual unemployment rate by educational attainment, 2023.

Source: Bureau of Labor Statistics

Housing Cost Burden vs. Median Income

Relationship between median household income and the share of renters who are cost-burdened (spending more than 30% of income on housing) across U.S. metropolitan areas.

Source: American Community Survey 5-year estimates
